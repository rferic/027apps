# Tareas

Shared tasks as post-its. A group holds tasks; each user also has a personal group.

- A task is **pending** or **done**, with one holder or none.
- Free: any member edits it and can take it. Held: only its holder edits and
  completes it. There are no creator privileges.
- One-off (optional due date) or periodic (weekly, monthly, yearly) with a history
  per period.
- A holder may release a task; another member may request it, and the holder
  accepts or denies from a push with buttons or from the app inbox.
- A group may enable **power**: tasks are worth 1–5 and whoever completes one earns
  them, in seasons that close into a results page.

See `docs/api.md`, `docs/settings.md` and `docs/events.md`. The full specification
is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-todo.md`.
